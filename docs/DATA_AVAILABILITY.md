# Data availability

The single-cell RNA-seq data analysed in this study were first reported in
Li et al. (Frontiers in Immunology, 2026; doi:10.3389/fimmu.2026.1813107) and
were not generated for this study.

Peripheral blood mononuclear cells from three healthy controls and four
patients with myasthenia gravis were profiled with the Singleron 5' single-cell
RNA-seq system (microwell chip), sequenced on an Illumina NovaSeq 6000
(150 bp paired-end) and processed with CeleScope
(https://github.com/singleron-RD/CeleScope).

Raw sequencing reads are deposited in the Genome Sequence Archive for Human
(GSA-Human), National Genomics Data Center, China, under accession HRA011723
(BioProject PRJCA040830), and are available through a Data Access Committee in
accordance with the Regulation on the Management of Human Genetic Resources of
the People's Republic of China.

Processed count matrices are provided at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22959640 for reproducibility; they are not the
primary data archive.
